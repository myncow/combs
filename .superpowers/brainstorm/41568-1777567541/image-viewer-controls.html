Modal viewer + inline actions

Clicking the image or the inspect button opens a larger viewer with zoom controls, while download stays visible on the card for quick access.

B. Simpler: image-only modal + footer actions

Simpler modal

Fewer controls and less UI chrome, but inspecting details takes an extra click and zooming would likely rely on browser scaling only.

What both options add

  • Click image to inspect at larger size
  • Dedicated download action
  • Clear separation from regenerate

Main tradeoff

  • A is richer and more useful for close inspection
  • B is lighter, but less capable when the image has small details
